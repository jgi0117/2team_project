Create one polished, highly legible desktop web dashboard screenshot for a Korean predictive maintenance and spare-parts planning student project. This is a visual design example for a repository README, not a working app and not actual model results.
Landscape composition approximately 1600x1000. White/off-white surface, navy typography, restrained teal and amber accents, spacious grid and large Korean text with excellent spelling. No computer frame, no perspective, no photos, no decorative illustrations.
Header exact title: "설비 예지보전 · 정비 의사결정". Strong visible banner directly below: "화면 예시 · 모든 예측·비용 수치는 가상입니다".

Two middle panels:

Right panel title "정책별 총비용 비교". Horizontal bars with consistent lengths and exact labels: "고장 후 수리 1.5억", "정기 교체 1.2억", "예측 기반 정비 0.9억". Small subtitle "동일 평가기간 · 가상 시뮬레이션". The third bar is teal, others muted slate. No claim of real savings or proven 42-day optimum.
Bottom wide panel title "경고 후 대응 계획". Three rows with columns "설비", "고장 위험", "재고", "대응 상태", "권장 조치".
